I built this site as the live, canonical version of my resume and a place to write about what I'm actually seeing in AI/ML day to day — new tooling, agentic workflows, and lessons from building production ML/LLM platforms.

A few practical notes on how this site works, in case you're curious:

- The [resume page](/resume/) is synced automatically from my master CV — it's never hand-edited here, so it should always be current.
- The [projects page](/projects/) is a hand-curated set of write-ups on the larger systems I've built.
- This blog is just markdown files — new posts show up here automatically as I write them.
